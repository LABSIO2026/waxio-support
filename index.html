What the app does

WAX.IO gives you two decks and nothing else: a pitch fader, the record under your finger, and no waveforms, no BPM readout, no sync indicator. You learn to hear when two tracks drift apart and to bring them back together by hand.

Getting started

  • Put on headphones — matching by ear without them is much harder.
  • Tap + on a deck to load a track from the Files app.
  • Start deck A: that is your reference beat. Start deck B and listen for the drift.
  • Move the pitch fader to change the speed; nudge the record to line the beats up.

Frequently asked

Where is the waveform? There isn't one, on purpose. Every other DJ app shows you the answer, so the ear never trains. WAX.IO removes the visual crutch.

Which audio formats work? Anything the Files app can hand to the system, including AAC/M4A, MP3, WAV and AIFF.

Does it need an internet connection? No. The app works entirely offline and makes no network calls.

Are my files uploaded anywhere? No. Your audio files are opened locally and stay on the device.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the app, go to the purchase screen and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account used to buy them.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

WAX.IO collects no personal data, contains no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and does not transmit anything off the device. There is no account and no sign-in.
